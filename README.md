# Facts-Only Mutual Fund Assistant

A lightweight factual retrieval tool for retail mutual fund queries built for Groww users, grounded on SBI Mutual Fund schemes.

## Product Selected
- Platform: Groww
- AMC: SBI Mutual Fund
- Schemes Covered: 
  1. SBI Bluechip Fund (Large Cap)
  2. SBI Long Term Equity Fund (ELSS)
  3. SBI Flexicap Fund (Flexi Cap)

---

## Disclaimer Snippet
> Disclaimer: Facts-only. No investment advice. This assistant provides verified public information strictly from AMC and AMFI documents. It does not provide financial planning, scheme recommendations, or return projections.

---

## Known Limits
- Scope is currently restricted to SBI Mutual Fund equity offerings.
- Does not compute real-time NAV or historical CAGR returns (links directly to official factsheets instead).
- Refuses all subjective queries (e.g., "Which fund should I buy?").
- No PII is collected or processed.

## Sample Q&A

**Q1: What is the expense ratio of SBI Bluechip Fund?**  
**A:** The Net Expense Ratio for SBI Bluechip Fund (Direct Plan) is approximately 0.85% as per the factsheet. Total Expense Ratios vary based on direct vs regular plans.  
*Source:* https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/equity-schemes/sbi-bluechip-fund  
*Last updated from sources: October 2026*

**Q2: What is the lock-in period for ELSS?**  
**A:** SBI Long Term Equity Fund (ELSS) has a statutory mandatory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of allotment of each unit. Investments qualify for tax deductions under Section 80C.  
*Source:* https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/equity-schemes/sbi-long-term-equity-fund  
*Last updated from sources: October 2026*

**Q3: What is the minimum SIP for SBI Flexicap Fund?**  
**A:** The minimum Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) amount for SBI Flexicap Fund is ₹500 per month (minimum 12 installments) or ₹1,000 per month (minimum 6 installments).  
*Source:* https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/equity-schemes/sbi-flexicap-fund  
*Last updated from sources: October 2026*

**Q4: What is the exit load for SBI Bluechip Fund?**  
**A:** An exit load of 1% is applicable if units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year from the date of allotment. Nil exit load applies thereafter.  
*Source:* https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/equity-schemes/sbi-bluechip-fund  
*Last updated from sources: October 2026*

**Q5: Should I invest in SBI Bluechip Fund or ELSS for the highest return?**  
**A:** I only provide verified scheme facts and cannot offer investment advice, recommendations, or portfolio reviews. Please consult a SEBI-registered advisor or review mutual fund fundamentals at AMFI Investor Education.  
*Source:* https://www.amfiindia.com/investor-corner
